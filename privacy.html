Privacy Notice

How DU2BAO2 may collect, use, disclose, secure and retain personal data.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 boleh memproses maklumat akaun, profil, penyenaraian, laporan, komunikasi dan data teknikal untuk mengendalikan serta melindungi platform. Maklumat awam dan maklumat peribadi hendaklah dipisahkan. Pengguna boleh meminta akses atau pembetulan melalui saluran privasi yang akan diterbitkan sebelum pelancaran.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this marketplace will be [DU2BAO2 LEGAL ENTITY]. The registered entity, registration number, business address and privacy contact must be inserted before public launch.

2. Personal data we may process

  • Account data: email address, account identifier, display name and authentication status. When Google sign-in is selected, Google may provide basic profile information such as name, email address and profile image according to the permissions shown during sign-in.
  • Guest-mode data: a temporary browser-session flag and locally saved wishlist information. Guest mode does not create a DU2BAO2 seller account and cannot be used to submit listings.
  • Seller data: legal/contact details supplied for verification, seller profile information and account history.
  • Listing data: product descriptions, prices, locations, photographs, delivery terms and seller declarations.
  • Communication and report data: complaint details, moderation messages and evidence voluntarily submitted.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser information, logs, cookies or local-storage identifiers.
  • Transaction-related records: information users provide to support a complaint, even though DU2BAO2 does not process the buyer’s payment in the current model.

3. Sources of personal data

We may obtain data directly from users, from their use of the website, from reports made by other users, from service providers supporting the platform, and from lawful public or official sources where verification or safety review is reasonably necessary.

4. Why we use personal data

  • Create and protect accounts.
  • Publish and manage listings.
  • Enable buyer–seller contact.
  • Review sellers, listings and reports.
  • Prevent fraud, counterfeit listings, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain records and comply with lawful obligations.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Respond to access, correction, complaint or support requests.

5. Public and private information

A seller’s display name, general location, listing details, photographs and selected contact method may be visible to visitors. Full identity documents, private account records and home-address information should not be displayed publicly unless a lawful disclosure requirement has been properly assessed and implemented.

6. Service providers and disclosures

The website may rely on hosting, database, authentication, storage, security and domain-service providers. Current technical files indicate the use of Supabase, Google sign-in, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Data may also be disclosed to professional advisers, payment/courier providers involved in a user’s complaint, regulators, courts or law-enforcement authorities where permitted or required.

7. Retention and record keeping

Personal data should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, legal obligations, safety, dispute handling and record keeping. Marketplace supplier and advertisement records may require a defined retention period under applicable Malaysian electronic-trade rules. A final retention schedule must be adopted before launch.

8. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used, including access controls, Row Level Security, secure authentication, moderation permissions, backups and incident procedures. Password and Google authentication are handled through Supabase Auth and the selected identity provider; DU2BAO2 should never request or store a user’s plain-text password or Google password.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to or correction of their personal data, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, object to certain processing, or ask questions about retention and disclosure. Requests should be sent to the privacy contact below.
