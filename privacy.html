Privacy Notice

How DU2BAO2 may collect, use, disclose, secure and retain personal data.

Draft last updated: 31 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 memproses maklumat akaun, penjual, penyenaraian, laporan, komunikasi dan data teknikal untuk mengendalikan serta melindungi platform. Maklumat penjual yang ditandakan sebagai awam boleh dipaparkan pada penyenaraian, manakala rekod peribadi dihadkan kepada penjual dan pentadbir yang dibenarkan. Rekod tertentu boleh disimpan sekurang-kurangnya tiga tahun jika dikehendaki; data lain tidak patut disimpan lebih lama daripada yang perlu.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this marketplace will be [DU2BAO2 LEGAL ENTITY]. The registered entity, registration number, business address and privacy contact must be inserted before public launch.

2. Personal data we may process

  • Account data: email address, user identifier, display name, authentication status and basic profile information received from Google when that sign-in option is selected.
  • Guest-mode data: a temporary browser-session flag and locally saved wishlist information.
  • Public seller disclosure: seller or business name, seller type, public email, public telephone/WhatsApp, business or service address, website and business name.
  • Restricted seller record: legal name, private contact number, state, country, business registration number where supplied, the optional last four characters of an identity reference, declaration version and timestamp. This version does not request a full MyKad or passport image.
  • Listing data: product details in Bahasa Malaysia and optional English, photographs, price, location, payment methods, delivery estimate, sale terms, certification information and declaration timestamp.
  • Reports and moderation: report reason, details, reporter contact, evidence link, listing snapshots, status, administrator handling data and listing audit records.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser information, logs, cookies or local-storage identifiers where used by the hosting, authentication or security services.

3. Sources of personal data

We may obtain data directly from users, from their use of the website, from reports made by other users, from service providers supporting the platform, and from lawful public or official sources where verification or safety review is reasonably necessary.

4. Why we use personal data

  • Create, authenticate and protect accounts.
  • Publish seller disclosures and approved listings.
  • Enable buyer–seller enquiries, availability checks and offers through prepared WhatsApp messages.
  • Review sellers, listings, reports and moderation history.
  • Prevent fraud, counterfeit listings, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain records and comply with lawful obligations.
  • Respond to access, correction, complaint or support requests.
  • Improve website reliability and user experience.

5. Public and restricted information

Approved listings may show the seller or business name, seller type, public email, public telephone/WhatsApp, business or service address, website, listing details and photographs. The legal name, private telephone number, business-registration record, optional identity-reference fragment and declaration record are stored separately and are intended to be accessible only to that seller and authorised DU2BAO2 administrators through Row Level Security. Sellers should use a suitable business or service address where possible and should not enter information in public fields that they do not want displayed.

6. Service providers and disclosures

The website may rely on Supabase for database, storage and authentication, Google for optional sign-in, GitHub Pages for hosting and Cloudflare for domain services. Data may also be disclosed to professional advisers, relevant service providers, regulators, courts or law-enforcement authorities where permitted or required. DU2BAO2 does not sell passwords or request a user’s Google password.

7. Retention and record keeping

Seller, advertisement/listing, complaint, moderation and audit records are configured with a three-year retention review date to support applicable marketplace record-keeping requirements. The date is not an automatic deletion job. DU2BAO2 must adopt and operate a documented review, preservation and secure-deletion process before launch. Records may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for an unresolved dispute, fraud prevention, a legal claim or a lawful request. Other personal data should be deleted or anonymised when no longer necessary.

8. Security

DU2BAO2 uses separate public and private database tables, Supabase Row Level Security, authenticated administrator checks, restricted storage rules and account authentication. No internet service can promise perfect security. Users should protect their accounts, and administrators should grant access only where required, review access regularly and keep service-role keys out of browser files.

9. Access, correction, withdrawal and complaints

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to or correction of personal data, ask questions about processing, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, or raise a privacy complaint. Some data may still be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for safety, dispute handling or legal claims. The working privacy contact must be inserted before public launch.
