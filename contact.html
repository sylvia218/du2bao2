1. Product and transaction questions

Contact the seller through the product listing for questions about condition, authenticity evidence, price, payment, delivery, meet-up, warranty, cancellation or returns. DU2BAO2 does not have possession of the item.

2. Reporting a listing or user

Use the report function on the relevant listing where available. Include a clear reason and only the evidence needed for review. Do not publish identity documents, full bank details or private addresses in public fields.
