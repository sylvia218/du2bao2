Soalan mengenai barang boleh dihantar melalui pilihan Hubungi Penjual pada halaman produk. Aduan boleh dibuat melalui butang Lapor Penyenaraian. Permintaan akaun atau privasi hendaklah dihantar melalui maklumat hubungan rasmi DU2BAO2 yang mesti dilengkapkan sebelum pelancaran awam.

1. Product, availability and offer questions

Use Contact seller on the product page, then choose Ask a question, Check availability or Make an offer. DU2BAO2 prepares a WhatsApp message containing the listing link. Confirm condition, authenticity evidence, price, payment, delivery or handover, warranty, cancellation and return terms with the seller.

2. Reporting a listing

Use Report this listing on the relevant product page. Select the closest reason, provide a follow-up email and factual details, and add an optional safely hosted evidence link. Do not submit passwords, bank PINs, one-time codes or full identity documents. Authorised administrators can review and update the report status.
