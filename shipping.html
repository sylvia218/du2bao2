Shipping Information

How delivery works when a DU2BAO2 buyer and seller arrange the transaction directly.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 tidak mengurus penghantaran. Penjual perlu menyatakan kaedah penghantaran, caj, lokasi, anggaran masa penghantaran dan pilihan pengambilan sendiri. Pembeli hendaklah mengesahkan semua butiran ini dengan penjual sebelum membuat bayaran.

1. Direct buyer–seller arrangement

DU2BAO2 currently provides listing, account and communication features. The seller chooses whether to offer courier delivery, same-day delivery, self-collection or an in-person meet-up. DU2BAO2 is not the carrier and does not collect shipping charges on behalf of the seller.

2. Information every seller should disclose

Before accepting a buyer’s payment, the seller should clearly state:

  • Delivery method: courier, self-collection, meet-up or another agreed method.
  • Shipping charge: the exact amount, whether it is included in the listed price, or how it will be calculated.
  • Dispatch time: for example, “ships within two working days after cleared payment.”
  • Estimated delivery time: a reasonable estimate rather than a guaranteed arrival date.
  • Delivery area and restrictions: including whether delivery is limited to Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah or Sarawak.
  • Tracking and insurance: whether these are included and who pays any additional charge.

3. Confirm before paying

The buyer should obtain written confirmation of the final item price, shipping charge, delivery method, seller’s return arrangement and expected dispatch date. Messages should be kept until the transaction is complete.

4. Packing, tracking and proof

Sellers should package items appropriately, retain photographs of the packed item and provide a tracking reference when available. Buyers should record visible parcel damage before opening and keep the packaging if a complaint may be needed.

5. Self-collection and meet-ups

Meet in a public, well-lit place where the buyer can inspect the item. The buyer should not be pressured to confirm acceptance before a reasonable inspection. For high-value goods, both parties may agree to meet at an appropriate professional inspection or authentication location.

6. Delay, loss or damage

Because the delivery contract is arranged directly, the buyer should first contact the seller and, where relevant, the courier or payment provider. DU2BAO2 may review reports, remove listings or restrict accounts, but it does not automatically reimburse shipping loss or damage.

7. Applicable rights

A seller’s stated delivery policy does not remove rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Sellers should not use shipping terms to avoid responsibility for materially misdescribed or defective goods where applicable law requires a remedy.
