Prohibited Items

Products, services and conduct that are not permitted on DU2BAO2.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

Barang tiruan, curi, menyalahi undang-undang, berbahaya, dikawal selia atau melanggar hak pihak lain adalah dilarang. DU2BAO2 boleh menolak atau memadam penyenaraian, meminta bukti, menyekat akaun dan bekerjasama dengan pihak berkuasa.

1. General rule

A user must not list, request, promote or facilitate anything illegal, unsafe, fraudulent, counterfeit, stolen, restricted or inconsistent with these policies. The examples below are not exhaustive.

2. Counterfeit, stolen and deceptive goods

  • Counterfeit, replica, imitation or unauthorised branded goods.
  • Stolen goods or goods with removed, altered or suspicious identifying marks.
  • Fake receipts, certificates, authenticity cards, warranties or provenance documents.
  • Listings that use another seller’s images or materially misrepresent the actual item.
  • Goods sold by a person who lacks ownership or lawful authority.

3. Illegal, dangerous or regulated categories

  • Weapons, weapon parts, explosives and dangerous devices.
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances and unlawfully supplied medicines.
  • Tobacco, vaping, alcohol or age-restricted products unless DU2BAO2 expressly creates a legally reviewed category with suitable controls.
  • Protected wildlife, endangered-species products and goods requiring permits that the seller cannot lawfully provide.
  • Unsafe, recalled, contaminated or unlawfully modified products.
  • Counterfeit currency, official documents, identity documents or government-issued credentials.

4. Intellectual property and digital abuse

  • Pirated media, software, counterfeit licences or unauthorised digital access.
  • Goods or content that infringe trade marks, copyright, design rights or other intellectual-property rights.
  • Hacked, stolen or unlawfully transferred accounts, personal data, SIM registrations, banking credentials or access codes.

5. Prohibited services and conduct

  • Scams, fraudulent investments, illegal gambling, money laundering or deceptive payment arrangements.
  • Sexual services, exploitative content or unlawful adult material.
  • Harassment, threats, hate content, impersonation, doxxing or sale of personal information.
  • Requests to move a transaction for the purpose of evading safety controls, legal duties or account restrictions.

6. Evidence and moderation

DU2BAO2 may request proof of ownership, purchase, authenticity, safety, permit or business status. Failure to provide reasonable evidence may result in rejection or removal even where the item is not conclusively proven unlawful.

7. Reporting

Users should report suspected prohibited items through the platform. Do not confront a suspected scammer or attempt to recover goods in an unsafe way. Where a crime or immediate danger is suspected, contact the appropriate Malaysian authority.
