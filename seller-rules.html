Seller Responsibilities

What sellers must provide and how listings should be prepared before approval.

Draft last updated: 31 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

Penjual mesti memberikan maklumat identiti dan hubungan yang tepat, menggunakan gambar barang sebenar, memberi maklumat penyenaraian utama dalam Bahasa Malaysia, mendedahkan harga penuh, kaedah bayaran, anggaran penghantaran, syarat jualan dan kecacatan penting. Bahasa Inggeris boleh ditambah. Barang tiruan, curi atau dilarang tidak dibenarkan.

1. Seller identity and contact information

Before creating a listing, a seller must complete the public seller disclosure and restricted private account record. Public fields include the seller or business name, seller type, public email, public telephone/WhatsApp and business or service address. The restricted record includes the legal name, private contact number, state and country, with business-registration and a four-character identity-reference fragment optional. Do not upload a full identity document through the ordinary form.

2. Ownership and authority

The seller must own the item or have clear lawful authority to sell it. DU2BAO2 may ask for receipts, purchase records, photographs, serial information or other evidence. Sensitive details should be submitted privately rather than published in full.

3. Listing accuracy and language

  • Provide the main product title, description, payment methods, delivery or handover estimate and sale terms in Bahasa Malaysia.
  • An English title and description may be added for convenience.
  • Upload two to eight clear photographs of the actual item.
  • State the correct brand, category, condition, location and full asking price.
  • Disclose major scratches, damage, repairs, alterations, missing parts and non-original components.
  • State what is included and any relevant certification or safety information.
  • Do not hide material information in private messages after advertising the item differently.

4. Price and transaction disclosures

The listing form requires the full asking price, payment methods, estimated delivery or handover time, sale terms and relevant certification information. Sellers must also disclose any additional charge and the applicable cancellation or return arrangement before the buyer commits. Required disclosures must be available in Bahasa Malaysia; another language may be provided in addition.

5. Authenticity and brand claims

A seller must not claim that an item is authentic, original, certified or covered by a warranty without a reasonable basis. Words such as “replica,” “mirror,” “inspired” or “1:1” do not make counterfeit sales acceptable.

6. Enquiries, offers and fulfilment

Buyers may prepare a WhatsApp question, availability request or offer through the product page. The seller must respond honestly, clearly accept or reject any offer, confirm the final terms and perform any arrangement the seller accepts. Do not switch the item, payment recipient or important term after agreement without the buyer’s clear consent.

7. Records

Sellers should keep copies of listings, messages, accepted terms, payment, shipping or handover evidence and complaint material. DU2BAO2 is configured to retain seller, listing, moderation and report records with a three-year review date where applicable marketplace rules require that period. A longer period may apply for unresolved disputes or lawful requests.

8. Approval is not certification

Admin approval only means that a listing has passed the platform’s current review process. It is not an authentication certificate, ownership guarantee, safety certification or promise that the item will be delivered.

9. Reports and enforcement

Users can report a listing from its product page. DU2BAO2 administrators can review pending listings, reports and restricted seller records, and may reject, remove or restrict listings and accounts for incomplete disclosures, misleading information, prohibited goods, repeated complaints, evasion of controls or failure to provide reasonable evidence.
