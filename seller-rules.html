Seller Responsibilities

What sellers must provide and how listings should be prepared before approval.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

Penjual mesti mempunyai hak sah untuk menjual barang, menggunakan gambar sebenar, memberi maklumat tepat, mendedahkan kecacatan, harga penuh, kaedah bayaran, caj penghantaran, anggaran penghantaran dan syarat pemulangan. Barang tiruan atau curi adalah dilarang.

1. Seller identity and contact information

A seller must provide accurate identity and contact information requested by DU2BAO2. Public display and private record keeping should be separated. The final platform must be configured to collect and disclose the information required by applicable Malaysian electronic-trade rules while protecting personal data under the PDPA.

2. Ownership and authority

The seller must own the item or have clear lawful authority to sell it. DU2BAO2 may ask for receipts, purchase records, photographs, serial information or other evidence. Sensitive details should be submitted privately rather than published in full.

3. Listing accuracy

  • Use a truthful title and description.
  • Upload clear photographs of the actual item.
  • State the correct brand, model, condition and location.
  • Disclose major scratches, damage, repairs, alterations, missing parts and non-original components.
  • State what is included, such as box, receipt, accessories, warranty documents or dust bag.
  • Do not hide material information in private messages after advertising the item differently.

4. Price and transaction disclosures

Before a buyer commits, the seller should clearly provide the full item price, shipping and other charges, accepted payment method, terms and conditions, estimated delivery time, return arrangement and any relevant product-safety or certification information. Required disclosures should be available in Bahasa Malaysia; additional languages may also be used.

5. Authenticity and brand claims

A seller must not claim that an item is authentic, original, certified or covered by a warranty without a reasonable basis. Words such as “replica,” “mirror,” “inspired” or “1:1” do not make counterfeit sales acceptable.

6. Communication and fulfilment

Respond honestly, confirm the agreed terms, dispatch within the stated time and provide tracking where promised. Do not redirect a buyer to a different item, recipient or payment arrangement after the agreement.

7. Records

Sellers should retain listing, communication, payment, shipping and complaint records for an appropriate period. The platform may also be required to retain seller and advertisement records under applicable rules.

8. Approval is not certification

Admin approval only means that a listing has passed the platform’s current review process. It is not an authentication certificate, ownership guarantee, safety certification or promise that the item will be delivered.

9. Enforcement

DU2BAO2 may reject, remove or suspend listings and accounts for incomplete disclosures, misleading information, prohibited goods, repeated complaints, evasion of controls or failure to provide reasonable evidence.
