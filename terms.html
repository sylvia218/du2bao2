The rules for using DU2BAO2 as a buyer, seller or visitor.

Draft last updated: 31 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 menghubungkan pembeli dan penjual serta menyediakan penyenaraian, hubungan, semakan pentadbiran dan saluran laporan. Urusan bayaran, penghantaran atau penyerahan menggunakan pilihan yang tersedia dan dipersetujui oleh pihak berkaitan. DU2BAO2 tidak memegang wang pembeli, menyediakan escrow, mengurus penghantaran atau mengesahkan ketulenan secara bebas pada masa ini. Perkhidmatan tambahan boleh diperkenalkan kemudian di bawah terma berasingan.

1. Acceptance of these terms

By accessing or using DU2BAO2, you agree to these Terms, the Privacy Notice, Prohibited Items Policy, Seller Responsibilities and any listing-specific terms confirmed between the buyer and seller. Do not use the platform if you do not agree.

2. Eligibility

Users must be at least 18 years old, or use the platform with the valid consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian where permitted. A person who cannot lawfully enter the proposed transaction must not buy or sell through the platform.

3. DU2BAO2’s marketplace role

DU2BAO2 provides technology for listings, account access, search, seller contact, moderation and reporting. Unless expressly stated for a future service, DU2BAO2 is not the owner, seller, purchaser, agent, auctioneer, authenticator, courier, payment processor, escrow provider or party to the user transaction.

4. Accounts, guest access and seller records

Visitors may browse in guest mode without creating a seller account. Guest mode is temporary, is limited to browsing and locally saved wishlist information, and cannot be used to sell.

  • Provide accurate, current account and seller information and keep it updated.
  • Protect account credentials and do not share access.
  • Do not create accounts to evade a restriction or impersonate another person.
  • Sellers must complete the requested public disclosure fields and restricted private account record before listing.
  • Do not upload a full identity document unless DU2BAO2 later provides a specifically authorised secure verification process.

5. Seller obligations

  • Own the item or have lawful authority to sell it.
  • Use photographs of the actual item and disclose material defects, repairs, alterations and missing parts.
  • Do not list counterfeit, stolen, illegal, unsafe or prohibited goods.
  • Provide accurate seller identity/contact disclosures, full price, payment methods, delivery or handover estimate, sale terms and relevant certification information.
  • Provide the required listing and transaction disclosures in Bahasa Malaysia; an English version may be added for convenience.
  • Do not make false authenticity, warranty, ownership or condition claims.
  • Respond honestly to enquiries and offers and perform any terms the seller accepts.

6. Buyer obligations

  • Review the listing and ask questions before paying.
  • Confirm the seller, item, final price, payment method, delivery and return terms.
  • Do not misuse personal information or pressure a seller into unsafe arrangements.
  • Use lawful funds and do not attempt fraud, chargeback abuse or false reporting.
  • Inspect high-value goods and keep relevant transaction evidence.

7. Payment, shipping, cancellation and returns

In the current model, the available payment, delivery, handover, cancellation and return arrangements are confirmed between the buyer and seller. DU2BAO2 does not currently receive the item price, hold buyer funds, operate escrow, arrange delivery or automatically issue refunds. If supported transaction services are introduced later, their scope, fees and separate terms will be displayed before use.

8. User content and licence

Users retain responsibility for content they upload. By posting a listing, the user grants DU2BAO2 a non-exclusive licence to host, reproduce, format and display the content for operating, promoting and protecting the marketplace. The user must have the necessary rights to the content.

9. Moderation, removal and suspension

DU2BAO2 may review, reject, edit visibility, remove or preserve content and may warn, restrict or suspend accounts where there is suspected illegality, safety risk, policy breach, fraud, counterfeit activity, repeated complaints or a lawful request. Approval of a listing is not a guarantee of authenticity, ownership, safety or seller reliability.

10. Complaints and reports

Users may use the Report this listing form to submit a reason, details, follow-up email and an optional safely hosted evidence link. DU2BAO2 may record, review and update the status of a report, request further information and take marketplace moderation action. Submitting a report does not automatically prove wrongdoing or create a refund.

11. Disclaimers and limitation

The platform is provided on an “as available” basis. DU2BAO2 does not guarantee continuous access, successful sales, seller performance, buyer payment, delivery, condition or authenticity. Nothing in these Terms excludes liability or statutory rights that cannot legally be excluded.

12. Ending use of the platform and retained records

A user may stop using the platform. DU2BAO2 may suspend or terminate access for serious or repeated breaches, security concerns, legal requirements or risk to users. Seller, listing, audit, complaint and moderation records may be retained for at least three years where applicable rules require it, or longer where reasonably necessary for legal claims, safety or lawful requests. Other personal data should not be kept longer than necessary.

13. Governing law

These Terms are intended to be governed by the laws of Malaysia. The final dispute clause, registered entity and service address should be reviewed by Malaysian counsel before public launch.

14. Contact

See the Contact Us page. The final terms must identify the registered DU2BAO2 entity and a working support address.
