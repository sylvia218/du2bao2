The rules for using DU2BAO2 as a buyer, seller or visitor.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 ialah platform yang menghubungkan pembeli dan penjual. DU2BAO2 bukan pemilik atau penjual barang, bukan pihak kepada urus niaga, tidak memegang bayaran, tidak menyediakan escrow dan tidak menjamin ketulenan. Pengguna bertanggungjawab terhadap akaun, kandungan, penyenaraian dan urus niaga mereka.

1. Acceptance of these terms

By accessing or using DU2BAO2, you agree to these Terms, the Privacy Notice, Prohibited Items Policy and any listing-specific terms agreed directly between buyer and seller. Do not use the platform if you do not agree.

2. Eligibility

Users must be at least 18 years old, or use the platform with the valid consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian where permitted. A person who cannot lawfully enter the proposed transaction must not buy or sell through the platform.

3. DU2BAO2’s marketplace role

DU2BAO2 provides technology for listings, account access, search, seller contact, moderation and reporting. Unless expressly stated for a future service, DU2BAO2 is not the owner, seller, purchaser, agent, auctioneer, authenticator, courier, payment processor, escrow provider or party to the user transaction.

4. Accounts and guest access

Visitors may browse in guest mode without creating a seller account. Guest mode is temporary, is limited to browsing and locally saved listings, and cannot be used to sell, access a seller dashboard or obtain verification status.

  • Provide accurate, current information and keep it updated.
  • Protect account credentials and do not share access.
  • Do not create accounts to evade a restriction or impersonate another person.
  • Notify platform support of suspected unauthorised access.
  • DU2BAO2 may require additional verification for safety, legal or moderation reasons.

5. Seller obligations

  • Own the item or have lawful authority to sell it.
  • Use photographs of the actual item and disclose material defects, repairs and missing parts.
  • Do not list counterfeit, stolen, illegal, unsafe or prohibited goods.
  • Provide accurate price, charges, payment method, delivery estimate, return terms and required seller information.
  • Do not make false authenticity, warranty or condition claims.
  • Respond honestly to buyer questions and complete agreed obligations.

6. Buyer obligations

  • Review the listing and ask questions before paying.
  • Confirm the seller, item, final price, payment method, delivery and return terms.
  • Do not misuse personal information or pressure a seller into unsafe arrangements.
  • Use lawful funds and do not attempt fraud, chargeback abuse or false reporting.
  • Inspect high-value goods and keep relevant transaction evidence.

7. Payment, shipping, cancellation and returns

These matters are agreed directly between buyer and seller. DU2BAO2 does not receive the item price or automatically provide refunds. The Shipping Information and Cancellation & Return Policy explain the current marketplace model. Listing terms cannot remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. User content and licence

Users retain responsibility for content they upload. By posting a listing, the user grants DU2BAO2 a non-exclusive licence to host, reproduce, format and display the content for operating, promoting and protecting the marketplace. The user must have the necessary rights to the content.

9. Moderation, removal and suspension

DU2BAO2 may review, reject, edit visibility, remove or preserve content and may warn, restrict or suspend accounts where there is suspected illegality, safety risk, policy breach, fraud, counterfeit activity, repeated complaints or a lawful request. Approval of a listing is not a guarantee of authenticity, ownership, safety or seller reliability.

10. Complaints

Users may report listings or accounts through the available complaint channel. DU2BAO2 may request evidence and take marketplace action. Transaction refunds and legal remedies remain matters for the buyer, seller, payment provider, courier, regulator, tribunal, court or law-enforcement authority as applicable.

11. Disclaimers and limitation

The platform is provided on an “as available” basis. DU2BAO2 does not guarantee continuous access, successful sales, seller performance, buyer payment, delivery, condition or authenticity. Nothing in these Terms excludes liability or statutory rights that cannot legally be excluded.

12. Ending use of the platform

A user may stop using the platform. DU2BAO2 may suspend or terminate access for serious or repeated breaches, security concerns, legal requirements or risk to users. Certain records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

13. Governing law

These Terms are intended to be governed by the laws of Malaysia. The final dispute clause, registered entity and service address should be reviewed by Malaysian counsel before public launch.

14. Contact

See the Contact Us page. The final terms must identify the registered DU2BAO2 entity and a working support address.
