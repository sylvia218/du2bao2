Cancellation & Return Policy

A clear framework for seller-selected return terms without promising DU2BAO2 refunds.

Draft last updated: 31 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

Penjual mesti menyatakan syarat pembatalan, pemulangan dan bayaran balik yang berkaitan sebelum pembeli meneruskan. DU2BAO2 tidak memegang bayaran pembelian atau mengeluarkan bayaran balik secara automatik pada masa ini. Jika perkhidmatan pembayaran atau escrow diperkenalkan kemudian, terma perkhidmatan tersebut akan digunakan.

1. Current cancellation and refund model

DU2BAO2 does not currently hold the purchase money, operate escrow or control the seller’s payment account. The buyer and seller confirm the applicable cancellation, return and refund arrangement before proceeding, subject to applicable law and any payment-provider rules. A future supported payment or escrow service may have a separate process and terms.

2. Seller-selected return arrangement

Each listing should state one clear arrangement, such as:

  • No change-of-mind returns.
  • Returns accepted within three days after confirmed delivery.
  • Inspection and acceptance during meet-up only.
  • Return accepted only where the item is materially different from the listing.
  • Manufacturer warranty remains until a stated date, subject to the manufacturer’s own terms.

The seller should also state who pays return shipping, the required condition of returned goods and any reasonable evidence needed.

3. Cancellation before dispatch

A buyer who wishes to cancel should contact the seller immediately. The seller should confirm whether the item has already been dispatched, whether any non-refundable delivery cost has been incurred and when an agreed refund will be made.

4. Counterfeit, materially different or defective goods

A listing must not misrepresent authenticity, ownership, condition, included accessories or major defects. If the buyer reasonably believes the item is counterfeit, stolen, materially different or materially defective, the buyer should preserve the listing, messages, payment record, parcel evidence and item photographs, then report the listing to DU2BAO2.

5. Suggested dispute steps

  1. Contact the seller calmly and state the requested outcome.
  2. Provide relevant photographs, messages and proof of payment or handover.
  3. Allow a reasonable time for the seller to respond.
  4. Use any available payment-provider or courier dispute process.
  5. Use Report this listing on the product page for DU2BAO2 moderation review.
  6. Consider the appropriate Malaysian consumer, tribunal or law-enforcement channel where necessary.

6. What DU2BAO2 may do

DU2BAO2 may request information, preserve relevant platform records, remove content, restrict accounts or cooperate with lawful authorities. A marketplace moderation decision is separate from a court, tribunal, bank or payment-provider decision about money.

7. Mandatory legal rights

Nothing on this page is intended to exclude rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded. A seller cannot make false or misleading statements lawful simply by writing “no refund.”
