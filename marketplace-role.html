Marketplace Role

A plain-language explanation of what DU2BAO2 does and does not do in Stage 1.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 menyediakan platform penyenaraian dan komunikasi. Pembeli dan penjual membuat urus niaga secara terus. DU2BAO2 bukan penjual, bukan pembeli, tidak memegang wang, tidak menyediakan escrow, tidak mengurus penghantaran dan tidak menjamin ketulenan barang.

1. What DU2BAO2 provides

  • A website where approved second-hand listings can be displayed.
  • User accounts and seller profiles.
  • Search, filtering, product pages and saved listings.
  • A way for buyers to contact sellers.
  • Listing review, reporting and account moderation.

2. What DU2BAO2 does not currently provide

  • Ownership or possession of the seller’s item.
  • A platform checkout or collection of the item price.
  • Escrow, guaranteed payment or automatic buyer protection.
  • Courier, delivery, storage or fulfilment services.
  • Guaranteed authentication, valuation or condition inspection.
  • A promise that a seller will perform or a buyer will pay.
  • Automatic cancellation, return or refund processing.

3. Who forms the transaction

The buyer and seller decide whether to proceed and agree the item, price, payment, delivery, inspection and return terms. Their agreement is separate from the user’s agreement with DU2BAO2 to access the platform.

4. Platform moderation

DU2BAO2 may review content and complaints, remove listings and restrict accounts. These actions help operate the marketplace but do not turn DU2BAO2 into the seller or guarantee the transaction.

5. Future services

If DU2BAO2 later introduces payments, delivery, authentication, commissions, subscriptions or buyer protection, the website must clearly describe the new service, fees, responsibilities, eligibility and separate terms before presenting it as active.
