Marketplace Role

A plain-language explanation of what DU2BAO2 does and does not do in Stage 1.

Draft last updated: 31 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

DU2BAO2 menyediakan penyenaraian, carian, akaun, hubungan penjual, semakan pentadbiran dan saluran laporan. Pilihan bayaran, penghantaran atau penyerahan yang tersedia disahkan antara pembeli dan penjual. DU2BAO2 tidak memegang wang pembeli, menyediakan escrow, mengurus penghantaran atau mengesahkan ketulenan secara bebas pada masa ini. Perkhidmatan tambahan boleh diperkenalkan kemudian di bawah terma berasingan.

1. What DU2BAO2 provides

  • Marketplace browsing, search, categories and wishlists.
  • Email and Google account access, plus guest browsing.
  • Public seller disclosures and restricted seller account records.
  • Seller listing submission and dashboard tools.
  • Administrator listing review, complaint review and seller-record access controlled by Row Level Security.
  • A prepared WhatsApp flow for questions, availability checks and offers.
  • A Report this listing form and report-status workflow.

2. What DU2BAO2 does not currently provide

  • Ownership or possession of the seller’s item.
  • Platform checkout or collection of the item price.
  • Escrow, guaranteed payment or automatic buyer protection.
  • Courier, delivery, storage or fulfilment services.
  • Independent authentication, valuation or condition inspection.
  • A promise that a seller will perform or a buyer will pay.
  • Automatic cancellation, return or refund processing.

3. Buyer and seller arrangements

The buyer and seller use the available contact tools to confirm the item, price, payment, delivery or handover, inspection and return terms. Their arrangement is separate from their agreement with DU2BAO2 to access the platform. If DU2BAO2 later offers a supported payment, escrow or delivery service, the service will be identified and governed by additional terms.

4. Platform moderation

DU2BAO2 may review content and complaints, remove listings and restrict accounts. These actions help operate the marketplace but do not turn DU2BAO2 into the seller or guarantee the transaction.

5. Future services

DU2BAO2 may later introduce supported payments, escrow, delivery, third-party authentication, commissions, subscriptions or buyer-protection features. A feature must not be presented as active until its provider, scope, fees, eligibility, handling procedure and applicable terms are implemented and displayed.
