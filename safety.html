Safety & Complaints

Practical steps for safer buying, selling and reporting on a direct-deal marketplace.

Draft last updated: 27 July 2026
Pre-launch draft: This page is a practical website template, not a substitute for advice from a qualified Malaysian lawyer. DU2BAO2 must insert its registered legal entity, business address and working support/privacy contact details before public launch.

Ringkasan Bahasa Malaysia

Semak penyenaraian, identiti penjual, harga, kaedah bayaran, penghantaran dan syarat pemulangan sebelum membuat bayaran. Simpan bukti transaksi. Laporkan penyenaraian atau akaun yang mencurigakan kepada DU2BAO2 dan pihak berkaitan jika perlu.

1. Before agreeing to buy

  • Check the seller’s account, listing history, photographs and description.
  • Ask for clear photographs of defects, serial markings and included accessories where relevant.
  • Be cautious of pressure, extreme urgency, inconsistent identity details or a price that appears implausible.
  • Confirm the final price, all charges, payment recipient, delivery time and return arrangement in writing.
  • For high-value goods, consider an appropriate independent inspection or authentication service.

2. Payment safety

DU2BAO2 does not collect the purchase payment in the current model. The buyer should verify that the payment recipient matches the agreed seller and understand the bank or payment provider’s protections before transferring money. Never share passwords, one-time codes or remote access to a device.

3. Meet-up safety

Choose a public, well-lit place and tell a trusted person where you are going. Inspect the item before confirming acceptance. Do not carry out a meet-up where either party feels pressured or unsafe.

4. Keep useful evidence

  • A copy or screenshot of the listing.
  • Buyer–seller messages and agreed terms.
  • Payment record and recipient details.
  • Courier tracking and parcel photographs.
  • Photographs or professional findings relevant to authenticity or condition.

5. How to lodge a platform complaint

  1. Open the listing or seller profile and use the available report function.
  2. Choose the most accurate reason and describe what happened.
  3. Provide only relevant evidence and avoid publishing private information publicly.
  4. Respond to reasonable follow-up questions from platform support.

6. What a complaint may lead to

DU2BAO2 may review the account and listing, request information, hide or remove content, preserve records, warn or restrict a user, or cooperate with lawful authorities. The platform does not promise that every complaint will produce a refund or prove a criminal offence.

7. Other channels

A buyer or seller may also need to contact the relevant bank, payment provider, courier, brand owner, authentication provider, insurer, consumer authority, tribunal or law-enforcement authority. DU2BAO2’s moderation process does not replace those channels.
